# Business Questions

This document lists the 10 business questions addressed in the Retail
Sales Analytics project using PostgreSQL and SQL.

1.  **Top Customers and Revenue Contribution**\
    Who are the top 10 customers by revenue, and what percentage of
    total business revenue does each contribute?

2.  **Product Category Revenue Contribution and Ranking**\
    How much revenue does each product category generate, what is its
    percentage contribution to total revenue, and how do the categories
    rank?

3.  **Customer Lifetime Revenue and Purchasing Value**\
    Which customers generate the highest historical revenue, how many
    delivered orders have they placed, and what is their average order
    value?

4.  **Customer Segmentation Using Spending, Frequency and Recency**\
    How can customers be grouped into high-value, regular, occasional,
    lapsed, and never-purchased segments based on their purchasing
    behaviour?

5.  **Top 3 Products by Revenue Within Each Category**\
    Which three products generate the highest revenue within each
    product category?

6.  **Most Profitable Products and Gross Margin Analysis**\
    Which products generate the highest gross profit and gross margin
    after accounting for product costs?

7.  **Discount Effectiveness and Profitability Analysis**\
    How do different discount ranges affect revenue, units sold, gross
    profit, and gross margin?

8.  **Delivery Performance and Late Delivery Analysis by Region**\
    What are the average delivery times and late-delivery percentages
    across states, and which regions require further investigation?

9.  **Sales Channel Performance and Profitability Comparison**\
    How do online and physical-store sales channels compare in revenue,
    delivered orders, unique customers, average order value, gross
    profit, and gross margin?

10. **Data Quality Audit and Validation**\
    Are there duplicate customer records, missing order details, orders
    without items, or return quantities greater than the purchased
    quantities?
