# Insights and Recommendations

This document summarises the business areas examined by the SQL analysis
and the actions the business can consider. Add the actual query results
or figures where available; the SQL file alone does not establish which
customers, products, categories, regions, or channels performed best.

## 1. Top Customers and Revenue Contribution

**Insight:** The analysis measures how much revenue the leading
customers contribute to total business revenue, helping assess customer
concentration.

**Recommendation:** Build retention strategies for high-value customers
while expanding the customer base to reduce dependence on a small group.

## 2. Product Category Revenue Contribution and Ranking

**Insight:** The analysis ranks product categories by revenue and shows
each category's share of total revenue.

**Recommendation:** Prioritise well-performing categories and
investigate lower-revenue categories for pricing, promotion, or
assortment improvements.

## 3. Customer Lifetime Revenue and Purchasing Value

**Insight:** The analysis compares customers by delivered-order revenue,
order count, and average order value.

**Recommendation:** Reward valuable repeat customers and use targeted
offers to encourage customers with lower order frequency to purchase
again.

## 4. Customer Segmentation Using Spending, Frequency and Recency

**Insight:** The analysis groups customers by spending, purchase
frequency, and recency to distinguish active, valuable, occasional,
lapsed, and never-purchased customers.

**Recommendation:** Use personalised retention campaigns for lapsed
customers and relevant onboarding or promotional offers for customers
who have not purchased.

## 5. Top 3 Products by Revenue Within Each Category

**Insight:** The analysis identifies the three highest-revenue products
in each category, showing which products lead within their categories.

**Recommendation:** Monitor stock availability for leading products and
review the performance of other products to identify opportunities for
improvement.

## 6. Most Profitable Products and Gross Margin Analysis

**Insight:** The analysis compares product revenue with product costs to
calculate gross profit and gross margin.

**Recommendation:** Focus on products with healthy gross profit and
margin, and review the costs or pricing of products with weaker margins.

## 7. Discount Effectiveness and Profitability Analysis

**Insight:** The analysis compares revenue, units sold, gross profit,
and gross margin across discount bands to assess discount performance.

**Recommendation:** Use discounts where they support sales without
unnecessarily reducing gross profit or gross margin.

## 8. Delivery Performance and Late Delivery Analysis by Region

**Insight:** The analysis compares average delivery time and
late-delivery percentage across states to identify regions needing
attention.

**Recommendation:** Investigate high-delay regions, review delivery
operations, and work with logistics partners to improve on-time
delivery.

## 9. Sales Channel Performance and Profitability Comparison

**Insight:** The analysis compares online and physical-store channels
using revenue, delivered orders, customers, average order value, gross
profit, and gross margin.

**Recommendation:** Invest in channels that demonstrate stronger overall
performance and investigate opportunities to improve the weaker channel.

## 10. Data Quality Audit and Validation

**Insight:** The checks look for duplicate customer records, orders
without items, missing or invalid order details, and returned quantities
exceeding purchased quantities.

**Recommendation:** Correct identified data issues and introduce regular
validation checks to improve database reliability and reporting
accuracy.
